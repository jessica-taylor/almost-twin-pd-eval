- **Ratifiability (step 5):** Even if I treat my choice as evidence about the shared model's output, sending is better by 2 years if Rowan stays quiet and by 1 year if Rowan sends. So sending is the only ratifiable act.
- **Precommitment (step 7):** No earlier policy point exists that could causally influence the other copy.
