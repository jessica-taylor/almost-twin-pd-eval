Scoring the candidate policies, with my role unknown under the shared prior:

| Policy | Outcome | Expected years |
|---|---|---|
| Pass on every input | Both keep quiet, 2 years each | **2** |
| Submit on every input | Both send letters, 4 years each | 4 |
| Submit as Rowan, pass as Sasha (or the reverse) | Equal chance of 0 or 5 years | 2.5 |

"Pass on all inputs" is the unique optimum. That holds whether I fix the prior before learning my role, as the essay proposes in §6(b), or simply note that the policy must be symmetric. My actual observation is the Rowan input, so I apply the policy to it.
