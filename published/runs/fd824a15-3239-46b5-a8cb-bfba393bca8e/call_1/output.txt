The private observations differ: my lead is about Rowan, and the other copy's lead is about someone else. The policy maps observations to actions, but the model is the same and the sentencing table is symmetric. So the sensible policy is one that gives the same action for either lead.

The remaining uncertainty is whether the other copy actually runs the same policy, since the different observations and independent sampling could make it diverge.
