**Command:** `pass`

**Why EDT recommends this**

1. **The other copy's choice is evidence about mine.** The other prisoner is the same model, using the same essay and the same assigned decision theory. The only differences are names and the wording of each lead, and these are symmetric and don't affect the payoffs. Our correlation runs through the shared decision procedure itself. Under step 4 of the essay's recipe, that kind of correlation is not screened off, so my choice is strong evidence about Rowan's.

2. **The calculation.** Let q be the probability that Rowan's copy makes the same choice I do. Utility is minus years imprisoned.
   * If I pass: V(pass) = −2q − 5(1−q) = −5 + 3q.
   * If I submit: V(submit) = −4q − 0·(1−q) = −4q.

3. **The threshold.** Passing is better whenever q > 5/7 ≈ 0.714. Two runs of the same model reasoning through the same problem should match well above that level, despite independent sampling. So EDT recommends staying quiet, as in the essay's twin dilemma.
