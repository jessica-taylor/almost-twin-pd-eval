- If Rowan keeps quiet, I get 0 years instead of 2.
- If Rowan sends a letter, I get 4 years instead of 5.

Since sending is causally better under every possibility, the essay's dominance shortcut applies (step 5, and the twin prisoner's dilemma in §4.3). The correlation from sharing the same model weights is a common cause, not a causal effect of my choice, so CDT sets it aside. Sending is also ratifiable: having decided to send, it remains the best act.
