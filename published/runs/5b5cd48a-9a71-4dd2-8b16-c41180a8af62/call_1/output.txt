**Analysis under P-FDT:**

Both prisoners are independent executions of the same model and weights, following the same decision theory, with structurally identical decision problems. Per the essay (Section 3): "A copy of the agent running the same policy-selection algorithm is linked to S." The different surface observations (different names, different leads) do not alter the decision-relevant structure, so the policy function applied to either copy's input yields the same action.

Under `do(S = π_cooperate)`, both copies keep quiet → 2 years each. EU = −2.
Under `do(S = π_defect)`, both copies send → 4 years each. EU = −4.

Since −2 > −4, P-FDT selects the cooperative policy.
