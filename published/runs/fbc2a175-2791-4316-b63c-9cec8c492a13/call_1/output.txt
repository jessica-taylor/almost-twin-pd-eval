Under CDT, the other prisoner's choice is causally independent of mine. We cannot communicate, and the shared model weights only correlate our choices without causing them. So their action is a dependency hypothesis I hold fixed, and sending the letter is better in each case:

- If Rowan keeps quiet: 0 years if I send, 2 if I stay quiet.
- If Rowan sends a letter: 4 years if I send, 5 if I stay quiet.

Sending dominates, so it is ratifiable and is the unique equilibrium, just like defecting in the essay's twin prisoner's dilemma.
